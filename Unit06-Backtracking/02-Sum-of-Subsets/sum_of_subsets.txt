PURPOSE
Find every subset of the given numbers that sums to the target.

HOW THE CODE WORKS
1. weight[] holds the numbers, chosen[] records the take/skip decision for each item, 'total' is the sum of all weights.
2. findSubsets(i, currentSum, remaining): 'remaining' is the sum of items i..n-1.
3. If currentSum == target a subset is printed from chosen[0..i-1] and the branch ends (all weights are positive, so adding more would overshoot).
4. A branch is cut if all items are used, the sum is already too big, or the sum plus everything left still falls short of the target.
5. Otherwise two recursive calls explore 'take item i' and 'skip item i'.
