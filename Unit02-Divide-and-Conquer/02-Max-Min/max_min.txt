PURPOSE
Find maximum and minimum together with fewer comparisons than two separate scans.

HOW THE CODE WORKS
1. maxMin(arr, lo, hi, mx, mn) fills mx and mn, which are references to variables in the caller (that is how one function 'returns' two values).
2. One element: both are that element. Two elements: one comparison decides which is max and which is min.
3. Otherwise the range is split at mid, both halves are solved, and two comparisons combine the answers.
4. The global counter 'comparisons' is increased at every comparison and printed beside the 2(n-1) that a simple scan would need.

COMPLEXITY
T(n) = 2T(n/2) + 2 gives 3n/2 - 2 comparisons for n a power of two; O(n) time.
